Happy New Year (2014) is a Bollywood heist-comedy movie about a group of misfits who come together to take revenge on a wealthy businessman. They form a dance team and enter a prestigious dance competition in Dubai as part of their plan to steal diamonds hidden in a hotel. Along the way, they become a close team and learn the importance of friendship, teamwork, and believing in themselves.

Actors:

Shah Rukh Khan, Deepika Padukone, Abhishek Bachchan, Sonu Sood, Boman Irani, and Vivaan Shah.
